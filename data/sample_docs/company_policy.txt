Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Full-time remote work requires VP-level sign-off and is evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

Expense Reimbursement

All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of purchase. Claims above $500 require a receipt and manager approval. Travel expenses are reimbursed at actual cost; meals are capped at $60 per day during business travel.

Leave Policy

Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month, capped at 18 days per year. Unused leave up to 5 days can be carried over to the next calendar year. Sick leave is tracked separately and does not require advance notice.

Equipment Policy

New employees receive a laptop and a one-time $200 home office stipend within the first 30 days. Equipment must be returned within 14 days of employment ending.
